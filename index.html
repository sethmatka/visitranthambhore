Before you set out

A little local
know-how.

The best journeys start with a few good details. Here’s what to know before you arrive.

01

When to visit

The park’s main visitor season generally runs from October to June. Cooler months are pleasant; warmer months can bring more wildlife sightings near water. Check current zone and seasonal opening information before booking.

↗
02

Getting here

Sawai Madhopur is the nearest railhead, around 10–15 km from the park area. Jaipur is the nearest major airport, roughly 180 km away. Arrange your onward transfer in advance.

↗
03

Safari basics

Safaris take place in designated zones with limited seats. Permits and identity details are required, and sightings are never guaranteed. Book through authorized channels and follow your guide’s instructions.
